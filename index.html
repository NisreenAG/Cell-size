Mini FRQ • Construct an explanation

Two spherical cells have radii 2 µm and 4 µm. For a sphere, SA:V = 3/r.

A. Calculate both SA:V ratios. B. Explain how increasing size affects exchange efficiency. C. Predict the effective SA:V of the larger cell if membrane folds triple its effective area.

Key: A: 1.5 µm⁻¹ and 0.75 µm⁻¹. B: larger cells have less plasma-membrane area relative to volume, potentially limiting exchange per unit volume and increasing intracellular diffusion distance. C: 2.25 µm⁻¹. More membrane area can improve transport capacity, but outcomes also depend on transporters and gradients.
